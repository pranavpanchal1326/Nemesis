# 0050 — The sound library is synthesised at runtime, not recorded and not committed

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-26
- **Owner:** PROD
- **Blueprint:** §E12, §E3.4, §E13, §E7.4 · §6 (Principle #6, #8, #9)
- **Consumed by:** F16 (`docs/FRONTEND-PHASE-PLAN.md` §4, where it was reserved)

## Context

§E12 specifies a sound design in concrete, physical terms: an ambient bed that
cross-fades on the model's real clock, positional foley per defect type, and
**interaction foley "recorded from paper and clay"** — a stamp thud, a paper
slide, a pin push, a page turn, a shutter, a roller pass. Plus the merge cue
(three soft taps into one low thump) and the single struck metal note for
`safety_trigger_fired`.

There is no foley recordist on this project and no licensed sound library in it.
`docs/FRONTEND-PHASE-PLAN.md` §4 lists this as one of four asset classes with no
owner, and proposes: *synthesise deterministically and commit the opus.* It also
says, unusually for that table, that this is *"the deviation most likely to be
judged insufficient, and the ADR should say so plainly."*

This ADR takes the proposal and **changes one half of it**, for reasons that only
became visible when the phase started.

## Decision

**Every sound in this product is synthesised in the browser, at the moment it
plays, from a seeded generator that is committed as source. No audio file is
committed, downloaded, or shipped.**

Concretely:

- `src/sound/synth.ts` renders each cue into an `AudioBuffer` on first use, from
  a description in `src/sound/cues.ts` — a noise burst shaped by an envelope, a
  struck-bar partial stack, a filtered impulse. Deterministic: the same seed
  produces the same samples, so what a reviewer hears is what CI hears.
- `src/sound/graph.ts` is §E12's Web Audio graph — a master gain with the
  modal duck on it, and four buses beneath it.
- Nothing is fetched. `public/` gains no audio directory.

**Why this is stronger than committing the opus, rather than a corner cut.**
Committing generated audio means committing a build output — the exact thing
ADR-0047 refused for the clay city and ADR-0055 refused for the camera track.
A committed `.opus` is a binary nobody can review in a diff, that drifts from
the generator that made it the first time somebody edits one and not the other,
and that has to be regenerated by a tool the repository would then also have to
carry (an Opus encoder is not in this dependency tree and adding one to produce
files we already know how to produce at runtime is a dependency bought for
nothing). Rendering at runtime keeps the *source* as the artefact, which is the
rule this repository has applied four times now.

**Cost, stated plainly, because §6 Principle #8 requires it:**

**Synthesised paper is not paper.** §E12 asks for tactility — foley *recorded
from paper and clay* — and the whole point of that instruction is the grain of a
real object. A filtered noise burst with a fast envelope is recognisably a
paper-like sound and is recognisably not paper. Of the four deviations in
`docs/FRONTEND-PHASE-PLAN.md` §4, this is the one most likely to be judged
insufficient by somebody who cares about the direction, and they would be right
to. It is recorded in §E28 and in §44, not only here.

**A second cost, smaller and worth naming:** first playback of a cue costs a few
milliseconds of rendering on the main thread. Cues are rendered once and cached
for the session, and the longest — the ambient bed — is rendered as a short loop
rather than as a minute of audio.

## Alternatives considered

**Commit the opus, as §4 proposed.** Rejected above. Worth noting that the
proposal was written before ADR-0047 and ADR-0055 established the
generate-don't-commit rule twice more; the change here is consistency with a
principle the repository has since sharpened, not a disagreement with the
original reasoning.

**A free-licence sound library (freesound.org and similar).** Rejected on two
counts. The licences are per-file and heterogeneous — CC0 beside CC-BY beside
CC-BY-NC — and a civic product deployed by municipalities cannot ship a bundle
whose attribution obligations nobody has enumerated. And it is a third-party
media dependency, which `scripts/demo_imagery.py` already argued against on
exactly these grounds.

**No sound at all, and mark §E12 unbuilt.** The honest fallback, and it was the
alternative if the synthesis sounded wrong. Rejected because one of §E12's items
is not decoration and cannot be replaced by a visual: *"an operator can hear
where the problems are before seeing them"* is an affordance, and §6 Principle
#9 admits it for that reason. Dropping the whole section to avoid one weak cue
would lose the one clause that earns its place.

**Record it on a phone.** Genuinely considered — a stamp, a page turn and a
paper slide are things anybody can record in a kitchen. Rejected on the same
committed-binary grounds as the opus, plus the practical one: six cues recorded
badly in one room is a *worse* library than six synthesised consistently, and it
would still need an encoder in the dependency tree.

## Consequences

**Easy:** zero bytes of audio in the repository, nothing to licence, nothing to
fetch, and the air-gapped bootstrap (§6 Principle #6, Phase 29) is untouched.
A cue can be re-tuned in a diff a reviewer can actually read. Muted deployments
pay nothing at all: no `AudioContext` is created until somebody unmutes.

**Hard:** the tactility §E12 asks for is not there, per the cost above. And
sound becomes a thing this repository *computes*, so a cue that sounds wrong is
debugged by reading an envelope rather than by opening an editor — which suits
this team and would not suit a sound designer joining it.

**Commits us to:** §E3.4 as a checked rule rather than a taste. A synthesised
library makes it trivially easy to add a seventh, eighth and ninth cue, and
*"colour, motion, and sound each carry exactly one meaning, or none"* is the
constraint that stops a product from acquiring a soundtrack. F16 lands that
audit as a guard (`check-guards.ts`, `single-meaning`) in the same commit as the
library, deliberately, so the vocabulary can never be larger than the rule.

## Revisit when

A sound designer joins, or a licensed library is bought — at which point the
decision is exercised rather than reversed: `cues.ts` becomes a manifest of
files, `synth.ts` becomes the fallback for a deployment that ships without them,
and the graph, the buses, the duck and the audit do not change at all.
